Visual Scene Graph for PowerPoint. Extracts structured layout data from PPTX files, enabling AI agents to parse element positions, understand spatial relationships, and detect layout patterns. Exposes bounding boxes, z-order, and spatial relations that humans see but machines couldn't access. Built on python-pptx. MIT licensed.
